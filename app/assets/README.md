# Your own photo (optional)

Drop a photo here as `hero.jpg` and it appears under the title on the app's front page.
Put a one-line caption in `hero_caption.txt` (for example "Departures board at Changi T3, 2025").

Use a photo you took yourself. It makes the page feel personal and avoids licensing problems.
Landscape, about 1600 px wide and under 500 KB, works best.
